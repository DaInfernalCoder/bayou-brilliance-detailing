# Design direction

## Subject, audience, and single job

Bayou Brilliance is a Houston car detailing service for drivers who can see and feel when their vehicle needs careful attention. The page has one job: make it effortless to call or book a detail online.

## Compact visual system

### Color

- Bayou depth — `#0B302F`: primary brand field and header scrim
- Cypress ink — `#10201F`: body text and deepest contrast
- Rainwater — `#E5F0ED`: cool page background
- Chrome mist — `#F7FAF9`: bright surfaces and type on dark fields
- Sunflash — `#F2C84B`: primary action and inspection markers
- Reflection blue — `#4C99A0`: links, rules, and waterline accents

### Type

- Display: Archivo Black for broad, painted-sign headlines
- Body: Manrope for open, practical reading at phone sizes
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono for inspection labels, process status, and local-service details

### Layout thesis

The page alternates broad quiet fields with cropped photographic panels. A reflective S-curve crosses the hero like a bayou seen in a polished hood, then straightens into the four-stage service inspection path.

```text
┌──────────────── header scrim ────────────────┐
│ mark       sections          CALL  |  BOOK   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ headline + actions      / curved photo field │
│ proof language         /  reflective waterline│
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ three service lenses / observable work       │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ASSESS ━ CLEAN ━ REFINE ━ REVIEW             │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ finish details + FAQ + paired final actions   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Signature

The reflective waterline is the one expressive move. It represents Houston without using skyline clichés and represents detailing without a generic sparkle icon. Every other surface stays disciplined: crisp radii, restrained shadows, and plainly labeled actions.

## Critique and revision

The first concept leaned too close to the common dark-teal premium-service template. The revision moves most of the page onto cool rainwater and chrome surfaces, reserves deep bayou for the hero and final action, and makes the S-curve functional by connecting the hero image to the ordered inspection sequence. The yellow accent behaves like reflected Houston sun and only marks primary calls and real sequence points.
